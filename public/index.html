Send us a question ↗

Can more than one person use it?

Yes. Your administrator can approve individual accounts for your church team. Approved members can access all the notices saved in your church’s archive. Local drafts stay on the browser where they were created until saved or exported.

Will it change the way our notice sheet looks?

The current template uses two matching A5 sheets on landscape A4, with Montserrat text, a church header, services, notices, rota and footer. You can add your church’s logo and adjust text sizes. If you need a different format, get in touch before joining.

Does it write the notices for us?

No. Churchpress helps organise text you provide. It can suggest services and notices from an email, and you can edit everything manually. Review all wording, dates and the print preview before publishing.

What gets saved in the archive?

The editable sheet, including its notices, services, rota and size settings, is saved before printing or downloading. Pasted source emails and uploaded logos stay local and are excluded from the shared archive. Saved weeks can be reopened and exported again; PDF files themselves are not stored.

Can we sign up online?

Churchpress is £11.99 a month with a 30-day free trial. New churches are set up individually at the moment. Enquire about Churchpress to discuss your team and what you need. If your church already has accounts, use the sign-in link.
