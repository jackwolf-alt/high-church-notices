Website privacy

This page describes the public Churchpress website. It does not replace your church’s own privacy information for notices and rota details in the dashboard.

Browsing this website

This website does not add analytics, advertising trackers, cookies or browser storage. Fonts and images are served with the site. Our hosting provider, Google Firebase Hosting, may process technical request information such as your IP address when serving pages. See Firebase’s privacy information.

Trying the example archive

The sample notices are fictional. Searching or expanding them happens only in this page. Search terms are not sent to a server or saved.

Sending an enquiry

Enquiry links open your email app. Nothing is sent until you choose to send the email. Enquiries currently go to jackwolf@jackwolfproductions.com. Your email address and anything you include will be used to respond to your enquiry and discuss the service. Please do not send private church notices or rota details in a sales enquiry.

Using the dashboard

The dashboard is a separate signed-in app. Each church has its own database and approved member accounts. Editable sheets and rota details are shared with that church’s approved team when saved, printed or downloaded. Ask your church administrator about account access and the handling of your church’s records.

For questions about an enquiry or to request deletion of enquiry correspondence, email jackwolf@jackwolfproductions.com.
